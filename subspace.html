Your payment does not go to the admin until you confirm you have received access. No more getting kicked out and chasing refunds.

How a protected transaction works
1
You join a group and pay

Your money goes into Subspace escrow — not to the admin yet.

2
Admin gives you access

You confirm the login works. A 48-hour countdown starts.

3

Escrow is frozen. A dispute opens. Resolved in 24 hours or you get a full refund.
